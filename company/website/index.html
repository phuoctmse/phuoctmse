How it works

      
From alert to fix, with a human-grade safety net

      
Every action passes through the same four stages, so automation never touches production without proof.

    

    

      
Observe
Informers watch Pods, Events and Nodes. A debounced pipeline filters noise.

      
Diagnose
A compact snapshot is sent to a pluggable LLM that reasons about root cause.

      
Validate
Proposed actions are checked against whitelist, owner-guard, bounds and cooldown.

      
Remediate
Dry-run first, then execute with an audit entry and one-step rollback.

    

  






  

    

      
Safety first

      
Automation you can trust with the pager

      
AI agents are only useful in production if they are predictable. Guard rails are not an add-on — they are the architecture.

      
    
        
  • Whitelisted actions only. Anything outside the allow-list is rejected.
    • 
        
  • Bounded blast radius. Replica limits, owner checks, cooldowns and idempotency.
    • 
        
  • Full audit trail. Every decision and change is recorded.
    • 
        
  • Dry-run & rollback. Preview first, undo instantly.
    •
